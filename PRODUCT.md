# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two audiences, one screen:

- **Primary operators — AHA Business Development team.** They run evaluations: pull a Shopee store's data, score it across operational, business, content, and advertising dimensions, and produce a verdict. Their context is focused, repetitive work — entering data, calculating scores, reviewing breakdowns.
- **Secondary viewers — the partner (brand / seller).** The evaluation report ("Laporan Evaluasi Partner") is **presented to the partner**. So the same surface that serves as an internal tool also doubles as an external-facing, credibility-carrying artifact the BD team shows in front of a client.

The job: evaluate brand health quickly and accurately, then present the result in a way a partner trusts at a glance.

## Product Purpose

Store ICU (AHA SICU) is a brand-health evaluation system for Shopee e-commerce stores. It turns scattered store performance data into a structured, scored evaluation across four dimensions, surfaces a clear verdict, and renders it as a report the BD team can present to partners. Success looks like: a BD evaluator completes an evaluation with confidence in the numbers, and the partner-facing report reads as authoritative and clear without explanation.

## Brand Personality

Tegas, data-driven, presentation-grade. The interface leads with numbers and verdicts, keeps decoration minimal, and earns trust through precision and clarity rather than flourish. Because partners see it, it must feel credible and polished — not a raw internal tool, but also never showy. Anchored to the AHA brand: Primary Blue (#325FEC) and Dark Navy (#1D388B) on white, Manrope throughout.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI SaaS.** No gradient text, no endlessly repeated identical cards, no uppercase tracked eyebrow above every section, no hero-metric template.
- **Loud / over-colorful.** Colors must serve data (verdict states, score thresholds), not compete for attention. No decoration that distracts from the numbers.
- **Spreadsheet-stiff.** Data needs hierarchy and rhythm — never a wall of raw cells that feels like Excel.

## Design Principles

- **Numbers first, framing second.** The score and verdict are the headline; supporting detail earns its place around them.
- **Two scores, never conflated.** The partner score (✓/✗ tally) and the AHA Compatibility Score (final_score) are distinct values with distinct meanings — label and place them so neither is mistaken for the other.
- **Present, don't just report.** Every screen the BD team might show a partner should read as authoritative at a glance, without a verbal walkthrough.
- **Color is signal, not decoration.** Hue carries meaning (verdict, threshold, pass/fail); it is not sprinkled for visual interest.
- **Respect the AHA identity.** Work within the committed brand palette and Manrope; extend it deliberately, don't reinvent it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1 against its background; verdict/threshold colors must not be the only signal (pair with icon or label). Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` with a crossfade or instant fallback. Full keyboard operability for the evaluation forms and navigation. Light and dark themes both maintained.
