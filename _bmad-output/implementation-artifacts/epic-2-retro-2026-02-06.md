## What Went Well

### Team Adaptability

- Story 2.3 (first full-stack) required 2 full reviews (20 issues) — team adapted patterns and subsequent stories improved
- Mr. Door's philosophy: "First planning, PRD, and architecture is the start. There are always unforeseen problems. The important thing is the capability to adapt to reach the goal."

### Code Review Effectiveness
- 56 issues caught before production — system working as intended
- Recurring patterns identified and documented into checklists
- Issue count trended down after Story 2.3 spike: 20 → 10 → 8

### Test Infrastructure
- Grew from 31 backend tests to 138 total (backend + frontend)
- Established patterns: AsyncClient mocks, TanStack Query testing, SSE testing
- Zero regressions across all stories

### Technical Foundations
- SSE architecture (EventBroadcaster with asyncio.Queue per subscriber) replaced polling
- OIDC dual-auth pattern for service-to-service communication
- Advisory locking for sync concurrency control
